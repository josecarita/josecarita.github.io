[TAMBO](https://github.com/Harvard-Neutrino/TAMBO-MC) (Tau Air Shower Mountain-Based Observatory) is a proposed neutrino observatory in the Colca Valley, Peru. It would detect Earth-skimming tau neutrinos by observing the air showers they produce, using particle detectors placed on the slopes of a deep valley. The collaboration is led by Harvard University, and UTEC develops part of the detector hardware.

### My role

- Evaluated and tested a detector electronics module developed at UTEC, focusing on identifying and mitigating false signal readings.
- Attended technical briefings and research talks on detecting neutrinos with mountain-based air-shower observatories.
- Gained experience working inside a large international research collaboration and with detector instrumentation.

### Why it matters to me

Separating real events from noise in a detector front-end is the same problem I want to work on in biomedical instrumentation, for example rejecting artifacts in ECG or PPG acquisition.
